Summer

Sapporo

Located further in the North, Sapporo has the perfect weather for the Summer. The city comes alive in the Summer with a massive, lively outdoor beer festival in Odori Park. Feeling too hot? Try out the local favorite rich milk-based soft serve icecream. No matter how hot it is, its important to try to keep you cool. Eat your favorite dessert as you stroll through the vast Moernumo Park.

Spring

Kyoto

In the spring, plent of Kyoto's cherry blossom tree blooms. Where you can view in the Philosper's Path. The chilly,pleasent weather makes it the perfect time for a stroll. Festivals such as Miyako Odori, a famous, traditional dance festival performed by geiko and maiko as well as Yatsura Matsuri held at a shrine to pray for health and ward off evil sickness takes place.

Fall

Tokyo

Tokyo, Japan's busy capital, is a place lit brightly by lanterns and smiles. In the autmn, festivals ranging from Kichijōji Autumn Festival with up to 11 portable shrines parading in the midst of September to Mt. Takao Autumn Leaves Festival with music performances, food stalls, and beutiful autmn mountain scenery in Late October to mid-December

Winter

Kanazawa

Kanazawa's blistful winter is the best place to spend your Christmas. Play in the snow at the stunning snow-covered Enroku-en Garden or try out the delicous local delicacies such as snow crab, sweet shrimp, and yellowtail (buri) or walk through the evening lights watching the striking winter illuminations Kanazawa Castle Park. Don't forget to pack your winter coat! It's important to remain warm in this chilly wheather.
